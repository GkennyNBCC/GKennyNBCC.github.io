KPI BHER Definition NBCC Definition / Explanation
WIL Experiences – Registered The total number of students who registered for an upcoming WIL opportunity.

BHER note: If curricular WIL, count students registered for the course. If not curricular, count students actively seeking a placement. 		Actively Seeking
Students who are actively seeking with intention of securing a WIL experience within their program (mandatory or co‑op).

Students who have participated in WIL orientation in preparation of securing a WIL experience.
WIL Experiences – In Progress The current number of students who have started but not completed a WIL opportunity.

Should include opportunities where students have begun hours.
Should not include students who withdrew.
Report most up‑to‑date number. 		Offered, accepted or match made.

Students who have accepted an offer from an industry partner for a future WIL experience.

Students in the process of securing OR finishing a WIL opportunity.

Examples:
– Students participating in capstone project with a partner
– Students who have accepted a co‑op work term offer or secured a field experience
WIL Experiences – Completed The total number of students who completed all aspects of their WIL opportunity, regardless of WIL type. Completed
WIL experiences have ended; monitoring and assessments are done.
WIL Dropouts The number of students who do not complete the WIL opportunity they started. Co‑op only.
Students who accepted a work term but for any reason did not move forward with it.
First‑Time Faculty Participation The number of faculty who include WIL in their curricula for the first time. New faculty incorporating capstone projects or becoming a CI (Coordinating Instructor) in the academic year.
New WIL‑Integrated Courses The number of courses that include WIL as part of the curriculum for the first time. New programs with designated WIL.
Employers Outreached The number of unique employers who received information and/or an invitation to participate in a WIL program.

Includes employers who:
– receive our newsletter
– we speak to on the phone
– receive platform education
– we speak to/engage with at events 		Outreach touchpoints where employers receive WIL‑related information (email, phone, events, info sessions, platform education).
Employers Engaged The number of unique employers who engaged in delivering WIL opportunities to students.

Employers who post co‑op work terms or field experiences AND secure students, OR participate in capstone projects. 		Industry partners who directly support WIL delivery by hosting/co‑supervising students or contributing to capstones.
Collaborators Engaged The number of collaborators engaged in the project.

Collaborators support delivery in an in‑kind capacity (sponsorship, mentorship, curriculum advising, co‑developing content).

Includes post‑secondaries, not‑for‑profits, community groups, etc. Does not include host organizations. 		Chambers, business councils, PACs, other non‑host organizations supporting specific WIL project streams.
Increased Private Sector Participation The number of new private‑sector businesses engaged in the delivery of WIL programming during the selected month. Any for‑profit business we talked to that had never participated in a WIL experience before.
Expanded Private Sector Participation The number of private‑sector businesses that increased the number of WIL students they engaged during the selected month. For‑profit businesses that provided opportunities in the past and are doing it again this year.
Private Sector Businesses Who Financially Compensated Students The number of private‑sector business partners who provided financial compensation to WIL students for the first time during the selected month.

Compensation includes wages, salary, stipends, or honoraria for co‑op, field placement, or capstone experiences. 		New private‑sector employers providing paid WIL opportunities for the first time in the reporting month.
